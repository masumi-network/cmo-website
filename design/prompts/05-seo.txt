16:9 landscape, epic wide shot. The very top spire of the Transamerica Pyramid in mid afternoon. Cuso stands triumphantly on the tip of the spire planting a small white flag, other shorter skyscrapers below him, soft clouds and fog drifting below the top, the whole miniature city spread out far below, Bay Bridge in the distance. Sense of height and achievement: number one. Cuso small in frame, upper center.
