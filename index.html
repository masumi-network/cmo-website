Questions

Does it sound like AI?

No. Cuso studies your website, your products and your past marketing, then writes in your voice. If a piece sounds off, tell him and he changes it.

Do I approve posts?

You approve the plan, and nothing goes out before you do. After that Cuso runs it. You can change, pause or undo any piece by telling him.

Which channels does Cuso run?

Social media on X, LinkedIn, Instagram and Facebook. Google Ads and Meta Ads. Your website SEO. Your newsletter. TikTok is coming soon.

What does it cost?

Starter is $200 a month, Growth is $500 and Scale is $1,000. Pay yearly and you pay for ten months. Ad spend is not part of the subscription price.

Can I cancel?

Yes. You can change or cancel any time in Settings. Your plan runs to the end of the period you paid for.

What do I need to start?

Your website and your marketing goals. Cuso does the research from there.

Can I ask for something that is not in the plan?

Yes. Tell Cuso what you need, like a post for today or a short announcement. He does it, remembers it and adjusts the plan.
