# F02 · City scene

Last updated: 2026-10-13 · Status: v0 (art passes 1 to 8 done) · Launch blocker: real-phone performance, final polish

## What it does
- A miniature San Francisco in three.js (React Three Fiber).
- Time of day drives the sun, sky, fog, window lights, street lamps and bridge lights.
- Hills: Russian Hill (under the 06:00 rooftop), Nob Hill, Telegraph Hill, Twin Peaks (two peaks), Pacific Heights. Streets climb them straight up, never steeper than 0.36.
- Parks: Golden Gate Park, Twin Peaks, Pioneer Park on Telegraph Hill (Coit Tower), Dolores Park, with trails, 3D grass, brush and rocks. Grass only grows in parks.
- Street grid with raised sidewalks (real kerbs), crosswalks, SF acorn lamps, hydrants and street signs, and parks. Pastel Victorian row houses modelled in 3D in six variants (bays, cornices, brackets, porches, stoops, flat or gable roofs), ten paint schemes. Downtown in 1920s stone towers, glass towers and SoMa lofts.
- Life: 13 Teslas (9 sedans, 2 Cybertrucks, 2 Roadsters): 7 on two city block loops, 6 crossing the Golden Gate and the Bay Bridge, sailboats with wakes and a ferry on the bay, Alcatraz with its lighthouse.
- Landmarks: Russian Hill rooftop, Hayes Valley shop, Mission wall, Union Square billboard, Transamerica Pyramid, Coit Tower, Ferry Building, California St and Union Square cable cars, Embarcadero pier, Bay Bridge, Golden Gate, and a fog bank off the ocean.

## How it works
- `src/lib/terrain.ts`: `heightAt(x, z)`, a sum of smooth elliptical domes that are exactly flat outside their radius. Everything on the ground uses it, including chapter spots (`at()` in `day.ts`). Hill sizes keep every street under `MAX_STREET_GRADE` (tested); the steep tops sit in parks.
- `grid.ts`: streets, `PARKS` (whole blocks) and `groundAt(x, z)` (street, sidewalk, grass or block). A park closes the streets inside it: the street ends at the park's kerb line and the park's sidewalk runs across it (a T junction). `KERB` (0.14) is how far streets sit below the sidewalks. `LANE_MARKS` (stop 2.7 short of every junction) and `CROSSWALKS` (zebras at the junctions of Cuso's walking streets only) feed the ground mask.
- `terrain.ts`: `heightAt` is the sidewalk and block level; `surfaceAt` is the real ground (streets `KERB` lower, curb ramps where a crosswalk lands). Everything on a street (cars, cable car and rails, bridge-end roads, Cuso when he crosses, chapter spots through `at()`) stands on `surfaceAt`; buildings, trees, lamps and landmarks on `heightAt`. Cuso crosses junctions flush on the ramps and hops the kerb mid-block.
- `ground.ts`: the terrain mesh, a grid every half unit where each kerb line is doubled (one copy at sidewalk height, one at street height) so the kerb is a crisp step, plus one kerb mesh (concrete face, lighter top edge, darker gutter). Cells wholly under the bay are skipped (the water is opaque).
- `layout.ts`: pure and seeded. Blocks, houses, towers, trees, lamps. Buildings sit level with their highest corner (nothing sunk); the footing covers the drop on the downhill side. It keeps streets, landmarks (`CLEAR`) and every camera's line of sight (`blocksView`) free. Nothing near the pyramid grows taller than it.
- `house-models.ts` and `houses.tsx`: the row houses, modelled. Six front models (one per variant, measured from the Painted Lady photos: 0 blue-style bay right with columned porch, 1 three storeys bay left, 2 bay on corbels with a tall stoop, 3 dentil cornice and arched door, 4 bay over a garage, 5 three storeys with pediment windows) and two shared bodies (flat roof with a chimney behind the false front, or shingled gable with a fish-scale gable end), as instances scaled uniformly by the plot width. One material (`houseMaterial`): each face carries a paint slot, each pane a window id; `paletteOf` gives every house one of ten paint schemes (`PALETTES`), never the same as the two houses before it in its row. Glass fakes the sky by fresnel (horizon is the hour's fog colour), with blinds and side curtains on some panes. Night: about half the windows light up as whole windows. Fronts stand out up to `RELIEF_DEPTH` (0.36); beside Cuso's walking lines they are flattened (`reliefDepth`) so his arms clear them. Houses are 3.8 to 5.0 tall (`FACADE_ASPECT`).
- `materials.ts`: shader patches on `MeshStandardMaterial`. `houseMaterial` (paint slots, siding, shingles, fish-scale, glass, night windows); `downtownMaterial` for the towers; `downtownMaterial` also draws the Pyramid (part 5 is its aluminium crown); `towerMaterial` (limestone landmark walls); `groundMaterial` blends asphalt, sidewalk, block ground and lawn by the street mask (the lawn comes from `lawn.ts`).
- `facade.ts`: the older procedural window shader (now only Alcatraz) and `facadeNight`.
- Textures: `public/textures/*.jpg`, generated with Codex from `design/prompts/textures/`. The facade photo atlas and the glass texture are gone (houses and towers are modelled now).
- `city.tsx`: renders the layout (a few draw calls for the whole city), the shore sea wall (lowered where a street meets it) and the fog. Buildings have a footing below ground so slopes never show gaps. Fog is unlit and tinted by time of day.
- `towers.tsx`: downtown as one merged mesh. Each tower gets a style from its own data (`towerStyle`): stone 1920s (granite shop base, belt course, cornices at setbacks, a slate pyramid, stepped or flat crown with a water tank), glass (mullions, spandrels, glazed lobby, sloped top or parapet with a plant room) or a SoMa loft (brick or concrete, shops below, parapet, tank). Rooftop parts stay within `ROOF_MARGIN` (1) and under the Pyramid limit. Two stone crowns are floodlit at night.
- `lawn.ts` and `grass.tsx`: lawns. Colour at several scales (gold on sunny wild slopes, green in mown gardens and shade, darker by the kerb), trails (dirt on the wild hills, gravel in the gardens), gullies down from the summits. 3D grass: one instanced draw of tufts that take the lawn colour under them and fade out with distance (20 to 34 units, 12 to 20 in lite), wind in the vertex shader, no shadows cast. Coyote brush and rock outcrops on Twin Peaks and Telegraph Hill. Nothing grows on streets, trails, Coit's plaza, under trees or on Cuso's routes.
- `street.tsx`: street furniture, placed by `streetFurniture()` in layout.ts (seeded): 43 SF post-top acorn lamps on two opposite corners of each junction (none on walking-street corners, which are curb ramps), 17 white hydrants with a blue bonnet and 7 green street-name signs at walking-street junctions. All stand on flat sidewalk 0.3 in from the kerb, off ramps, trees, bridge roads, chapter views and more than 1.2 from Cuso's routes (`street.test.ts`; `collision.test.ts` counts them as solids). At night each lamp paints a light pool into the ground shader (`lampLight` in textures.ts, no extra draw). Road paint is matte off-white (about 0.6, under the bloom threshold) with worn patches and its own share of the mask, so no grey halo.
- `landmarks.tsx`: the Mission wall, Union Square, the Ferry Building and both bridges. Signs show the visitor's brand. Shared helpers (merged parts, landmark textures, materials) are in `landmark-kit.tsx`.
- `rooftop.tsx`: the 06:00 Russian Hill apartment, modelled with the row houses' kit and `houseMaterial` (cream scheme): stone base with the front door at x −14, two angled bays per wall on corbels, belt courses, a modillion cornice exactly on the collision ring (`ROOFTOP.cornice`), tar and gravel deck, coping, stair bulkhead with a door. One draw call. `ROOFTOP` is unchanged (Cuso's jump and the collision solids read it).
- `shop.tsx`: the 07:00 Hayes Valley coffee shop. A two-storey corner building (house kit, cream scheme) over a modelled timber shopfront in deep green: pilasters, stall risers, display glass with transoms, a recessed door, the brand on the fascia (`SHOP_SIGN`, `useSign`). A modelled café behind the glass (counter, espresso machine, shelves, tables, pendant lamps, plants) whose colours glow warm, more at night. Floor level with the top of its front sidewalk. Bistro table and A-frame on the sidewalk; the corner lamp is the city's acorn lamp (`Lamps` in street.tsx).
- `pyramid.tsx`: the 15:00 Transamerica Pyramid as one merged mesh (`pyramidGeometry`): 38 storeys of precast floor bands and mullion ribs with glass set back, stepped corners, the A-frame arcade and recessed lobby at the base, plumb wings (`WINGS`), an aluminium crown and spire, a red beacon (`BEACON`). It stands on a level plaza cut into the hill (`PLAZA` in terrain.ts) with retaining walls on three sides. `PYRAMID`, `SPIRE`, `FLAG` and the platform height are unchanged.
- `parts.tsx`: small shared pieces (`Box`, `useGlow`, `useBrand`).
- `cable-car.tsx`: the double-ended California St car (merged geometry, follows `heightAt`) and its rails with the cable slot. `CAR.step` is the running board Cuso rides at 18:00.
- `pier.tsx`: the 22:00 Embarcadero pier: instanced planks, pilings and bracing, lamp, laptop, mug.
- `traffic.tsx`: 7 cars on two clockwise loops (`LOOPS`), seeded, plus 6 bridge cars: out of the Yerba Buena tunnel or the Marin fog, over the bridge on the right-hand lane, one lap of the downtown or Pacific Heights loop, and back; they turn round only while hidden (inside the tunnel, or past z -282 in the fog). Five one-car-at-a-time spots (three junctions, two tight bends) keep cars apart. Cars keep a gap (per car length) and stop for Cuso, but ignore him when he is more than 1.5 above or below them. One instanced mesh per car model and material (22 draws); wheels are their own instances and roll by the distance each car really drove; a soft contact shadow under each car.
- Car models: `public/models/cars/*.glb`, made by `node scripts/convert-cars.mjs` from the raw files in `3d-assets-raw/` (gitignored). It installs pinned `@gltf-transform/cli` and `occt-import-js` outside the project, sorts parts into named materials, drops logos, normalises size and axes, simplifies each part to a fixed error in metres (keeping creases and material seams), then meshopt-compresses (70 to 120 KB a car). The error (4 mm paint, 5 mm tyres) is a tenth of a pixel in the closest shot; a tighter one only adds triangles nobody sees. Licences of the source models are not confirmed yet (launch blocker).
- `tree-models.ts`, `tree-textures.ts` and `trees.tsx`: four kinds grown from seeds (3 variants each): London plane (mottled peeling bark, open lumpy crown), ficus (smooth grey trunk, dense glossy dome), Monterey cypress (gnarled, leaning east, flat foliage pads), Canary palm (diamond leaf-base trunk, 26 arching fronds). Leaves are alpha-tested cards from a canvas leaf atlas, shaded as one soft mass, darker inside, a little backlight, wind gusts from the west. Two batched draws for every tree (multi-draw); beyond 26 units a tree switches to its far model (lite always uses it). Street trees stand in a cast-iron grate.
- `coit-tower.tsx`: fluted column, observation arches, crown, lobby, on a level stone plaza set into the hill (`coitSeat`). Windows glow at night.
- `bay.tsx`: the water (standard material with a shader patch: ripples, fresnel, sun glint, shallows and shore foam from a distance field baked at load, day to night colour), a planar mirror in full mode, the sailboats (lofted hulls, sail trim from a west wind, heel, wakes), the ferry, Alcatraz and Treasure Island. Boat routes are functions of time and tested to stay on water.
- `roads.ts`: the bridge decks and the four bridge-end roads (`ROADS`, `DECKS`), pure data and geometry, drawn by `Roads()` in `landmarks.tsx` (asphalt, lane marks, concrete: 3 draw calls). Every road starts level with its deck (`DECK_Y` 7), is as wide as the deck and a street (3), keeps the deck's dashed centre line in phase, and never gets steeper than `MAX_GRADE` (0.32). A road's profile is the latest descent its grades allow, rounded over 3 units (`descend`). Where it is high it is a slab on piers (never in a junction or on a cross street); where it is low, retaining walls down to the ground; parapets only where the ground beside it falls away. The asphalt uses the street texture's world mapping, so a road and the street it lands on match. The ends: Golden Gate south over its anchorage, round a bend over the shore onto the Marina street (z -36) at the x -67 junction; north on `MARIN_ROAD` (terrain.ts, cut and fill) over the ridges and out into the fog; Bay Bridge west down its side span (`bayDeckY`) to the SF anchorage at the sea wall, then a viaduct along z 14 that lands at the x 13 junction (east of the Union Square rails); east down its side span into the Yerba Buena tunnel portal. `ISLAND` copies the island dome in `bay.tsx` (tested to match).
- `atmosphere.tsx`: `lightingAt(hour)` every frame; also drives `facadeNight`.
- Post effects (full mode): bloom, tilt shift, a little saturation, vignette, ACES. Phones and weak machines get "lite": no mirror on the water, no tilt shift, 2x multisampling instead of 4x (thin car trim needs it). Check frame rate on a real phone.
- Performance: after the first frame, `Ready` in `scene.tsx` uploads every texture and compiles every shader (`compileAsync`, into a render target because the post effects draw into one and shaders differ by output) while the poster still shows. Production skips the shader error log (reading it stalls on each compile). So the first scroll through a chapter does not hitch. `PerformanceMonitor` drops the pixel ratio to 1 if the frame rate falls.

## Acceptance
- Layout tests: seeded, on land, streets free, landmarks and views clear (with real heights), pyramid is the tallest nearby, never on Cuso's spot.
- Terrain tests: rooftop on Russian Hill, rails and street-level places flat, every spot on the ground, every street at most 0.36 steep, each named hill has a summit.
- Ground tests: parks are whole blocks, open hilltops are parks, no grass on an open street or sidewalk, streets connected with no dead ends into grass, cars and Cuso never on park grass, trees never on the road, buildings never sunk and their footing covers the slope.
- Kerb tests: the street drops exactly `KERB` on asphalt and nowhere else, the mesh has a crisp step, crosswalks on the street with ramps where Cuso crosses, lane marks never through a junction, Cuso's feet on the real mesh.
- House tests: every variant finite, windows only on glass, inside its plot, front within `reliefDepth`, triangle budgets, neighbours never share a body colour.
- Tower, grass and street tests: rooftop parts inside the footprint and under the Pyramid limit; blades, brush and rocks only on park grass off trails and routes; lamps, hydrants and signs on flat sidewalk, off ramps and Cuso's routes.
- The canvas renders and the page reports `data-scene="ready"` (e2e). Screens for every chapter in `screenshots/`.

## Gotchas
- Sun azimuth: 0 = south, 90 = east, 180 = north.
- Screenshots in headless Chrome: it pauses requestAnimationFrame without input, so scripted scrolls do not update the scene. Move the mouse while waiting (see `docs/VERIFY.md`).
- Facade hash: only hash exact per-cell values. Interpolated normals or wall-plane coordinates make windows flicker per pixel (the hash amplifies tiny errors).
- `react-hooks/immutability` lint rule is off in `src/scene/**`: R3F mutates three.js objects in `useFrame` by design.
- three r186 removed soft PCF shadows; we use `shadows="percentage"`.
- Do not add `BrightnessContrast` to the post chain: on a real GPU it turned the near half of the Golden Gate black (found by toggling effects one by one; likely negative colour values before tone mapping). Headless Chrome does not show it.
- A negative number raised to a fractional power in geometry code gives NaN, and one NaN vertex blanks the whole screen through bloom. `bay.test.ts` checks every bay mesh for finite values.
- The water finds the sky dome by how `atmosphere.tsx` builds it (drawn first, inside-facing, uniforms `top` and `horizon`). If that changes, the water falls back to fog colours and the sky shows up in the mirror again.
- Close-ups for art work: in dev, `?cam=px,py,pz,tx,ty,tz` pins the camera (see `camera-rig.tsx`). It is ignored in production.
