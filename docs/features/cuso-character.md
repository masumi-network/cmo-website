# F03 · Cuso

Last updated: 2026-10-15 · Status: done

## What it does
- The real mascot (`public/models/cuso.glb`) is rigged in the browser and travels the city's streets between chapters: down from the rooftop, along the walking streets, up onto Union Square's roof, a big jump to the pyramid tip, onto the cable car and the pier.
- He moves at his own natural pace behind the scroll: he walks near places (feet locked to the distance walked, so they never slide), dashes the long street stretches like a cursor, and jumps onto roofs. If you jump far ahead (the rail, Ask Cuso) he blinks there like a cursor (a quick pop).
- A lead camera walks ahead of him at a 3/4 angle (we see his face as he comes towards us), following where he really is, then settles into the next chapter's shot once he arrives. When a building comes between them, the camera swings up around his eye on a critically damped spring (one lift value, never steeper than about 77°), and settles back down only after he has been clearly visible for 0.45 s (hysteresis). Line of sight is an exact test against every building (`sightClear` in `layout.ts`, the Pyramid's taper included). The camera rides with him on a spring, so a dash never jerks it, and his hops at a spot never move it (`boom.test.ts`). When he blinks ahead, the camera flies after him over the roofs, like a rail jump. The lead cam stands to his right as authored; when a wall stands between him and that spot and his left is clear, it eases round to his left (`stepSide`), and back once the right has been clear for 0.5 s.
- The arm is the guarantee under the boom: if a building still stands between his eye and the camera (the boom lags a dash, cannot lift over a tall block), the camera comes in along that line to just short of the wall in the same frame, and eases back out (`armPose`, `sightHit` in `layout.ts`). A full scroll through the day, desktop and phone, and a round of rail jumps put the camera inside a building in 0 frames (it was 14 times, up to 2.7 s, before).
- Standing, nothing moves him as a block: he breathes, shifts his weight, blinks, turns his torso toward your pointer, waves when he arrives, and cheers with a little hop when you approve something. The baked clips in the GLB are no longer used.
- Cars wait when he is in their lane.

## How it works
- `rig.ts`: turns Mesh0 into a `SkinnedMesh` at load time. Bones: root, hips, body, two arms, and thigh, shin, foot per leg. In the file the hip hand is fused to his hip (one closed surface); `freeHipHand` cuts it free at load: the wrist ring is split and both openings are capped, the shoulder stays joined, the forearm and fist ride `armR`. At rest the hand sits on the hip as before. The raised hand is the file's own, rigid on `armL`. The file's character is a mirror image of the brand art, so `rigCuso` mirrors the mesh (`MIRROR`): from the front the arrow tip points up and left, the thumbs-up hand is on the viewer's right. `HULL` describes the rest pose; the swinging arm goes a little outside it. Weights come from where each vertex sits on the flat character (`weightsAt`), with smooth falloffs, so limbs bend like rubber hoses. Left and right split in the real gap between the legs, the crotch and waist blend smoothly, and each foot is turned in by `TOE_OUT` (the shoes are modelled turned out about 48 degrees) so the toes point ahead. Idempotent (the GLTF scene is cached).
- `gait.ts`: all poses through the bones, blended by weight: walk cycle (pendulum legs, knee bend on the swing leg, flat feet, lowered arm swing (the raised arm drops only so far that its fist stays clear of his body), bob, sway, lean), dash, tucked jump, wave, cheer and a breathing idle with a pointer look. `STRIDE` and `SPEED` are tuned to his short legs.
- `routes.ts`: one path per move (chapter i to i + 1), each segment tagged walk, dash or jump. Each chapter has an approach from a walking street to its spot; between them, Dijkstra on the walking streets (`WALK_X`, `WALK_Z` in `grid.ts`). Street stretches walk 4 units at each end and dash in between. Jumps go over things: onto a tall building he leaps straight up beside the wall to a point above the cornice and parapet (eased: fast off the ground, slowing at the top), then hops over onto the roof; the pyramid jump ends just above the platform edge, then hops to the middle. He faces where he goes, and the camera at rest, on every spot (desktop and phone shots; `collision.test.ts` turns him to both). At 18:00 he waits on the sidewalk beside the cable car's step, not on it: on the step his head touches the car's roof, so he could not turn to the camera. The fixed headings that let him ride the step facing out (`FACE`, a `face` per move) were deleted on 2026-10-15. The waterfront walking line (x 29) runs on the street's west side (x 27.4), clear of the pier deck, the Ferry Building plinth and the Bay Bridge's west anchorage: on the east side he dashed 1.35 from the anchorage's wall and the lead cam had no room. At the 22:00 pier he crosses the street, then hops onto the deck. `sampleDay(distance)` puts all moves on one line through the day.
- `cuso.tsx`: scroll sets a target distance; Cuso closes the gap at the speed of the segment he is on (or blinks if it is over 70 units), drives the gait, blinks his eyes, and publishes where he is (`rig.travel`) for the camera and his speech bubble. The bubble sits on his arrow-tip side and is placed on screen so it never goes under the top bar or a card (`data-card`), or off the screen edge. On short screens with no room under a card it sits beside it; on narrow phones long lines wrap.
- `camera-rig.tsx`: blends chapter shots by Cuso's real progress, with the lead cam in between (handing over over the last 12 units, going round Cuso, not through him); the boom keeps the view clear and the arm keeps the camera out of walls (`sightClear`, `sightHit` in `layout.ts`); rail, pin and Ask jumps and his blinks fly the camera (`jump` in the store, `rig.blinks`, `flight.ts`).

## Acceptance
- Rig test (`rig.test.ts`): decodes the real `cuso.glb`, rigs and poses it on the CPU (idle, wave, cheer, jump, dash, walk phases, walk-to-dash blends) and checks no edge stretches into a sheet (freed arm included), neither fist sinks into his body, the cut leaves no new open edges, the shoes point within 5 degrees of his heading, and `HULL` still holds him.
- Collision test (`collision.test.ts`): his body (`HULL` in `rig.ts`, five boxes measured from the mesh, turned to where he faces) never overlaps a house, tower or landmark solid (rooftop walls, cornice, parapet, bulkhead; Union Square walls, cornices, parapet, billboard; pyramid body, wings, spire, flag; both cable cars; Ferry Building; pier deck and rail) and never goes under the ground, sampled along the whole day line in both directions (scroll, rail and Ask Cuso all move him along it; far jumps blink onto spots), plus turning to the camera and cheering on every spot. The pyramid spire never stands behind him in the 15:00 shot.
- Route tests: start and end on the spots, walk on land or the pier, on the ground, never through a building, no jumps in the path itself, walking (not dashing) near places, one continuous day line.
- Layout tests: chapter cameras never start inside a building.

## Known limits
- He is flat: from behind, the arrow outline is mirrored. The back of the body is painted dark (`shadeCuso` in `rig.ts`) so it reads as his back, not a flipped cursor, and the lead camera mostly shows his front.
- At 06:00 the sun is behind him. His shader adds a soft light from the camera (`cusoFill`, set per hour from the look, F13), so his face reads white, not grey, without lighting the roof.
- Walking streets have no traffic and clear sidewalks; other streets have cars.
